Folio turns paper documents and photos into clean PDFs that fit upload size limits, right on your phone. It has no internet permission, so your documents never leave the device.

Made for the moment a form says "PDF only, maximum 100 KB".

<b>Scan or import</b>
• Scan with the camera, with automatic edge detection.
• Import photos from your gallery, or share images to Folio from WhatsApp, Files or any app.
• Combine many pages into one PDF.

<b>Fix pages in seconds</b>
• Auto-crop and straighten tilted pages.
• Rotate, reorder, duplicate or delete pages.
• Make text easier to read with Auto, Grayscale or Black & White modes.

<b>Fit any size limit</b>
• Set a size limit anywhere from 50 KB to 50 MB, and Folio compresses the file to fit. If a limit is too small for the pages, it tells you and suggests what to try.
• Export as a PDF, or as JPG images (each image under the limit) when a portal wants photos instead.
• Useful for exam and job application forms, college admissions, scholarship portals, visa and passport uploads, bank KYC, office paperwork and email attachments.

<b>Private by design</b>
• No internet permission: Folio cannot upload your files, even by mistake.
• No account, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, no analytics.
• No watermark on your documents.
• Your scans are stored in the app's private storage until you export or delete them.

<b>Free and open source</b>
Folio is free software under the GPL-3.0 licence. The source code is public on GitHub, and the same app is available on F-Droid. There are no paid features and no subscription.

<b>Good to know</b>
• Works without a network connection, anywhere.
• Very small size limits mean lower image quality. If text looks soft, try Black & White mode, fewer pages, or a slightly larger limit.
• Folio does not do OCR (text recognition) yet.
