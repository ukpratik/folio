Folio: Doc Maker

Turn photos of documents into clean PDFs

Scan or import pages, fix them in seconds, and fit any upload size limit, like 100 KB for a form. Completely offline.

  • Scan or import with automatic edge detection.
  • Fix it fast: crop, rotate, reorder, and make text clearer.
  • Fit any size limit: pick 100 KB, 500 KB, 2 MB… and Folio does the rest. PDF or JPG.
  • Truly private: no internet permission, no account, no ads, no tracking.

Coming soon to F-Droid and Google Play. Meanwhile, the source code is on GitHub.
